About SmartLeadGen

SmartLeadGen is built by Sayad Md Bayezid Hosan, a tech entrepreneur based in Bangladesh who also runs Connect with Bayezid (a digital agency) and SmartGen, a free web-utility tools platform.

The idea started from a simple, repeated task: finding local businesses that could actually use a website, better SEO, or a marketing overhaul — and doing it by hand, city by city, took hours. SmartLeadGen automates that search using Google's own business data, so the manual part disappears and the outreach part gets more time.

What we believe

  • Real data beats guesses — that's why lead-finding runs on Google's Places API, not an AI model inventing plausible-sounding businesses.
  • Your keys and your data are yours — encrypted, isolated per account, never shared across users.
  • Pricing should reflect real running costs, not a number picked before anyone's used the product.
